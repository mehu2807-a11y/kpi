What data does this need?

Every run needs three things:

  • The anomaly — which metric moved, by how much, and over what time window (e.g. "Region X revenue, down 7.5%, July 4–10").
  • Structured drivers — other numbers that moved around the same time and might explain it (price, marketing spend, inventory), ranked by how strongly they correlate.
  • Evidence — real-world context: news articles, support tickets, internal memos that speak to why it happened.

You can provide this three ways: hand-write the exact JSON, use the guided form (no JSON knowledge needed), or paste anything you already have — notes, a CSV excerpt, an email — and let the AI reshape it into the right structure for you.

How to read these results
  • Confidence — how strongly the evidence supports the top explanation. It's computed from citation coverage and agreement across sources, not the AI's own self-rating.
  • Escalate — set to "Yes" when causes are close or evidence conflicts. Treat that as "verify before acting," not a final answer.
  • Hypotheses — ranked possible causes. Every one is required to cite specific drivers or sources it's based on — never an unsupported claim.
  • Structured actions — concrete next steps, each with an owner and a way to check if it worked.
  • Executive / Analyst / Operations tabs — the same finding, reworded for who's reading it.
  • Telemetry — the technical cost of this run: how many AI calls, tokens, estimated $ cost, and how long it took.
