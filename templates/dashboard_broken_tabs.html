Data Processing Pipeline

1

Data Ingestion & Formatting

Raw data (CSV, unstructured text, or direct integrations) is parsed. The system automatically identifies metrics, values, and timestamps without requiring strict schema definitions.

2

Anomaly Detection Gate

Time-series models scan the ingested metrics against historical baselines. Anomalies are flagged based on configurable primary and secondary z-score thresholds.

3

Cross-KPI Correlation Engine

When an anomaly is detected, the engine scans all other tracked KPIs for cascading effects, identifying leading and lagging indicators automatically.

4

LLM Synthesis & Root Cause Analysis

The raw statistical evidence is passed to the selected AI Engine (e.g., Llama, Gemini, OpenAI). The LLM synthesizes the data into a plain-English narrative, scores confidence, and generates actionable hypotheses.
